    Good to know

    Questions people ask

    Do I really not have to pay?

    Repairs are free. Parts are donated or cost a few pounds at most, and we always tell you first.

    What if my item can't be fixed?

    You still leave knowing why, and we can sort usable parts for recycling. About three in ten items fall into this group.

    Can I bring something large?

    Yes, if you can carry it in. We can't repair gas appliances, TVs or anything mains-wired into a building.

    Is the venue accessible?

    All three venues are step-free with accessible toilets. Tell us in the form if you need extra support.

    Design notes (for reviewers)

    Audience: Bristol households, especially people on tight budgets and the environmentally curious. Primary action: reserve a free repair slot.

    Brand rules: 1) Warm paper background with deep green for actions and orange only for emphasis. 2) Fraunces for headings, Inter for body. 3) Friendly plain-English copy, short sentences, one primary button per section. 4) Dashed "stitch" borders signal mending.

    Interactions: category filter (aria-pressed), session selector and validated demo form, FAQ disclosure. Built with plain HTML, CSS and JS: a single page needs no framework. Fonts: Fraunces and Inter via Google Fonts (OFL). No images used.
